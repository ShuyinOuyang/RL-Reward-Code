## Data

* `data/*.jsonl` are subsets of [TIGER-Lab/AceCode-87K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/AceCode-87K) (formerly AceCode-89K), released under the MIT License. The `inferences` field contains completions generated by CodeLlama-Instruct, Llama-3.1-Instruct, Mistral-Instruct-v0.3 and Qwen2.5-Coder models; the licenses of those models may apply to their outputs.
* HumanEval+/MBPP+ (Apache-2.0) and LiveCodeBench (`livecodebench/code_generation_lite`) are downloaded at evaluation time and are not redistributed here.
